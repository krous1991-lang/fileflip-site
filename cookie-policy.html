Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This page explains the current use of cookies and similar technologies on FileFlip (https://fileflipconver.com). Read it with our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website or a third party may store in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels, and browser identifiers, can also remember information or help measure activity.

2. Current FileFlip tools

FileFlip's PDF and image conversion operations are designed to run locally in your browser. The published conversion pages do not require an account or a FileFlip conversion-session cookie to process a selected file.

The website loads some processing libraries from a third-party content-delivery network. Your browser makes requests to retrieve those resources. The CDN or website infrastructure may process technical connection information under its own policies. FileFlip does not currently publish a complete inventory of cookies set by every visitor's browser, browser extension, or infrastructure provider, so this page does not claim that no technical cookies can ever be present.

3. Analytics and advertising status

FileFlip uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-1FL2CWNTS6) to understand aggregate website usage. The Google Analytics tag is loaded only after you actively select “Accept analytics” in the consent banner. If you select “Reject analytics” or do not make a choice, the tag is not loaded. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings control. Your choice is stored in your browser. Google Analytics may set cookies or process technical and usage information after you consent. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's processing.

Google AdSense advertisements are not currently displayed on FileFlip. An ads.txt file alone does not set an advertising cookie. If advertising is enabled in the future, this policy will be updated and the appropriate consent process will be implemented before ads are served where required.

4. Managing cookies

Use the “Cookie settings” control displayed on FileFlip pages to accept or reject analytics, or to change a previous choice. If you reject analytics, the Google Analytics tag remains disabled.

You can also view, block, or delete cookies through your browser's privacy settings. Blocking all cookies may affect some websites or third-party resources. Browser controls do not replace a consent-management interface where one is legally required for non-essential tracking.

5. Changes and contact

We may update this page if the tools, hosting arrangements, analytics, or advertising services change. For questions, contact Simonako080591@gmail.com.
