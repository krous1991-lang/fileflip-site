Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This page explains the current use of cookies and similar technologies on FileFlip (https://fileflipconver.com). Read it with our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website or a third party may store in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels, and browser identifiers, can also remember information or help measure activity.

2. Current FileFlip tools

FileFlip's PDF and image conversion operations are designed to run locally in your browser. The published conversion pages do not require an account or a FileFlip conversion-session cookie to process a selected file.

The website loads some processing libraries from a third-party content-delivery network. Your browser makes requests to retrieve those resources. The CDN or website infrastructure may process technical connection information under its own policies. FileFlip does not currently publish a complete inventory of cookies set by every visitor's browser, browser extension, or infrastructure provider, so this page does not claim that no technical cookies can ever be present.

3. Analytics and advertising status

As of November 10, 2026, the published FileFlip page code reviewed for this policy does not include a Google Analytics tag, and Google AdSense advertisements are not displayed. An ads.txt file alone does not set an advertising cookie.

If analytics or advertising services are activated in the future, this page will be updated with the relevant categories, providers, purposes, and available choices. Where required by law, non-essential cookies or similar technologies must not be activated before valid consent is obtained.

4. Managing cookies

You can usually view, block, or delete cookies through your browser's privacy settings. Blocking all cookies may affect some websites or third-party resources. Browser controls do not replace a consent-management interface where one is legally required for non-essential tracking.

5. Changes and contact

We may update this page if the tools, hosting arrangements, analytics, or advertising services change. For questions, contact Simonako080591@gmail.com.
