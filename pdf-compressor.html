About PDF Compression

This tool recreates PDF pages as compressed images. It can reduce the size of some documents, especially scanned PDFs. The result may have lower image quality and text will no longer be selectable or searchable.

Processing takes place in your browser. Your selected PDF is not uploaded to our server by this tool.
