Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FileFlip (https://fileflipconver.com) handles information when you visit the website or use its PDF and image tools. It should be read together with our Cookie Policy and Terms of Use.

1. Files selected for conversion

FileFlip's conversion tools are designed to process the files you select locally in your web browser. The website does not intentionally send those selected files to a FileFlip file-conversion server. The browser may still request the website itself and third-party resources needed to display or run the tools.

Local browser processing is not a guarantee that a file is secure in every circumstance. Your device, browser extensions, network, and other software may affect privacy. Avoid using shared devices for confidential documents, and keep an original copy of important files.

2. Information involved in visiting the website

When your browser requests a web page or a resource, the website host and relevant service providers may process technical request information such as an IP address, date and time, requested URL, browser type, device or operating-system information, and error or security logs. This information is generally used to deliver, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the website. The precise information retained depends on the hosting and infrastructure providers.

3. Analytics

FileFlip uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-1FL2CWNTS6) to understand aggregate website usage and improve the site. Google Analytics is not loaded unless a visitor actively accepts analytics in the on-site consent banner. If a visitor rejects analytics or makes no choice, the analytics tag remains off. Visitors can change their choice at any time using the Cookie settings control. Analytics data is processed by Google under its applicable terms and privacy information.

4. Advertising

As of the date above, Google AdSense advertisements are not displayed on FileFlip. The presence of an ads.txt file does not itself mean that advertisements or advertising cookies are active.

If advertising is enabled in the future, advertising providers may process information such as browser identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and cookie or similar-technology data to deliver, measure, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize advertisements. Before enabling advertising, FileFlip will update its disclosures and implement an appropriate consent-management process for the regions where it is required.

For more information, see Google's advertising information and Google Ads Settings.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies only after you accept analytics through the consent banner. If you reject analytics or do not make a choice, FileFlip does not load the Google Analytics tag. Your consent choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember it. You can change your choice using the Cookie settings control. The file-conversion operations themselves are designed to run in your browser and do not require an analytics cookie.

If non-essential analytics or advertising cookies are introduced, they should only be activated after the consent required by applicable law. See the Cookie Policy for the current description.

6. Third-party resources and links

Some tools load open-source processing libraries from third-party content-delivery networks. When your browser requests those resources, the provider may receive technical connection information. FileFlip does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers or external websites. Review their own policies when appropriate.

7. Data retention and security

FileFlip does not intentionally store the files you select for conversion on its own file-processing servers. Technical request and security logs may be retained by hosting or infrastructure providers according to their configurations and policies. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Residents of the European Economic Area may have rights under the GDPR. To make a privacy enquiry, contact us using the address below.

9. Children's privacy

FileFlip is not intended for children under the age specified by applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, its providers, or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
