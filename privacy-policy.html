Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

FileFlip provides browser-based tools for converting PDF pages to PNG images and combining images into a PDF.

Files you choose

Conversions are designed to run in your browser on your device. The files you select are not intentionally uploaded to a FileFlip file-processing server. Your browser may use memory and temporary local resources while a conversion is running. You can close the page and remove downloaded files from your device whenever you choose.

Information and hosting

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process basic technical information such as requests, IP addresses, and security logs as part of delivering and protecting this website. Please review Cloudflare’s privacy information for details about its services.

Cookies and advertising

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe relevant data use, cookies, and choices where applicable.

Children and third-party services

FileFlip is a general-purpose utility website. Some conversion libraries or other resources may be loaded from third-party services, which have their own privacy policies.
