1. File Processing

FileFlip provides browser-based tools for PDF and image conversion, including:

  • PDF to PNG

  • PDF to JPG
  • PDF Compressor
  • JPG to WebP

Files selected for conversion are processed in your browser. We do not intentionally upload your selected files to our own servers for conversion.

You remain responsible for keeping copies of your original files and for checking downloaded results.
